HOW TO INSTALL ON ANDROID

Because Groovd is distributed directly as an independent APK, Android will ask for standard confirmation. Follow these 3 simple steps:

[01] Download the APK File
+

Tap the DOWNLOAD APK button on this website or scan the QR code using your phone camera. Your browser (Chrome, Firefox, or Brave) will begin downloading groovd-v1.0.0.apk.

// FILE: groovd-v1.0.0.apk • Verify the download completes in your notifications drawer.
[02] Permit "Install Unknown Apps"
+

Tap the completed download notification. If this is your first time sideloading an app from your browser, Android will display: "For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source."

Tap Settings in that popup, and toggle "Allow from this source" to ON.

// NOTICE: This is standard Android security for all apps downloaded directly from websites rather than the Play Store.
[03] Complete Installation & Launch
+

Return to the installer screen and tap INSTALL. Once installed, tap OPEN to launch Groovd, build your Critic Dossier, and start logging releases.
